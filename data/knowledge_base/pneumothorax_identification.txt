DETECTION AND SIGNS OF PNEUMOTHORAX:
The pathognomonic radiological sign of pneumothorax is the visualization of the visceral pleural line, represented as a thin, sharp white line separated from the chest wall by radiolucent gas containing no bronchovascular lung markings. Expiratory views may accentuate subtle apical pneumothoraces. In supine or trauma radiographs, pneumothorax typically manifests as the 'deep sulcus sign', characterized by an abnormally deep, radiolucent lateral costophrenic angle. Tension pneumothorax is a clinical emergency featuring ipsilateral diaphragmatic flattening and contralateral tracheal/mediastinal shift.

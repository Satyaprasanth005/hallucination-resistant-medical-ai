RADIOLOGICAL EVALUATION OF CARDIOMEGALY:
Cardiomegaly on a standard posteroanterior (PA) chest radiograph is classically defined when the cardiothoracic ratio (CTR) exceeds 0.50 (50%). On anteroposterior (AP) portable radiographs, cardiac magnification frequently occurs due to beam divergence, causing pseudo-cardiomegaly. Associated findings of left ventricular enlargement include rounding and downward displacement of the cardiac apex. Right ventricular prominence leads to filling of the retrosternal clear space.
Differential diagnoses include pericardial effusion, dilated cardiomyopathy, and valvular heart disease.

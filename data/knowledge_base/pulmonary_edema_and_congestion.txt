RADIOGRAPHIC STAGES OF PULMONARY EDEMA:
Stage 1 (Vascular Congestion / Cephalization): Redistribution of pulmonary blood flow to upper lobes, manifesting as enlarged upper lobe pulmonary vessels exceeding lower lobe vessels.
Stage 2 (Interstitial Edema): Fluid transudation into interstitial septa produces Kerley B lines (short horizontal lines perpendicular to the lateral pleura at lung bases), peribronchial cuffing, and indistinct hilar margins.
Stage 3 (Alveolar Edema): Frank alveolar flooding producing bilateral perihilar bat-wing or butterfly distributions of fluffy, ill-defined opacities.

PULMONARY ATELECTASIS (VOLUME LOSS):
Atelectasis refers to incomplete expansion or collapse of lung parenchyma. Direct radiographic signs include displacement of interlobar fissures toward the collapsed region and increased local opacity. Indirect signs include elevation of the ipsilateral hemidiaphragm, mediastinal shift toward the collapsed side, and compensatory hyperinflation of adjacent lobes. Subsegmental or discoid (plate-like) atelectasis frequently presents as linear horizontal opacities in dependent lung bases post-operatively or with hypoventilation.
